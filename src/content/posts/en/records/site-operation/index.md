# Characteristics of Site Operation

DrLeeWorks is a personal website dedicated to documenting and sharing experiences, insights, and technical information gained through software development, computing, engineering, and research.

Rather than publishing static, one-time articles, this site aims to maintain **living records that are continuously refined and supplemented based on real-world usage and hands-on experience**.

## 1. Articles Are Continuously Updated

Articles on DrLeeWorks may be revised or expanded even after their initial publication.

When new software versions are released, when hands-on usage reveals new insights, or when errors or better approaches are discovered, **existing posts will be continually updated.**

Therefore, posts on this site are not considered final static endpoints, but rather **living documents that accumulate validated knowledge and findings over time**.

In particular, because the software and computing landscape evolves rapidly, information that was accurate at the time of writing may change over time.

## 2. Documenting Hands-On Experience and Practice

Whenever possible, articles go beyond merely summarizing third-party information to record firsthand insights gained through actual usage or development.

In software overview articles, this includes not only official features but also real-world characteristics, installation nuances, and practical usage tips discovered during testing.

Similarly, content related to engineering and research focuses on techniques, methodologies, and tools actively explored or applied in actual research workflows.

## 3. In-House Software Is Built with AI Agentic Vibe Coding

The in-house software published on DrLeeWorks is not intended as commercial-grade enterprise software, but rather as **hobbyist and experimental projects designed to implement personal ideas or solve specific practical needs**.

The development process actively adopts **AI Agentic Vibe Coding**, heavily leveraging AI coding agents throughout the pipeline.

Humans determine the desired functionality, operating logic, user interface, problem areas, and enhancement directions, while AI agents assist extensively in writing code, refactoring, debugging, and testing.

Consequently, programs shared on this site do not guarantee the same level of polish or stability as commercial software, and unexpected bugs or environment-specific issues may arise.

When issues are identified or superior solutions emerge through actual use, programs are updated with new releases, and corresponding articles are revised accordingly.

Documenting this iterative development journey itself is one of the core subjects DrLeeWorks aims to share.

## 4. Prioritizing Practical Records Over Exhaustive Manuals

The objective of this site is not to construct exhaustive reference manuals explaining every topic from start to finish.

Instead, records focus on genuinely needed information, practical techniques, and problem-solving workflows.

As a result, some articles may begin as concise usage notes and gradually evolve into more detailed documentation as additional experience is accumulated.

## 5. Potential Errors and Omissions

While every effort is made to maintain accuracy, articles are based on individual experiences and personal notes, which means errors or omissions may occur.

In particular, software versions, operating systems, and hardware configurations can cause identical procedures to produce different outcomes.

Whenever inaccuracies are identified or better methods are verified, existing articles will be promptly corrected and updated.

## 6. Software and Articles Continuously Evolve

Programs developed and published by DrLeeWorks may see changes to their features and user interfaces throughout their lifecycle.

New versions may be released to introduce features, resolve bugs, or improve usability, with documentation and download information updated in tandem.

Because earlier versions may differ from the descriptions in updated articles, it is always recommended to verify the program version during use.

The same principle applies to written posts. Rather than preserving published articles as immutable artifacts, the site favors revising and enriching existing content as new insights and experiences emerge.

## 7. A Space for Cumulative Knowledge

One of the most important objectives of running this site is **to prevent newly gained knowledge from being consumed once and forgotten, instead preserving it as cumulative records**.

Whether it is solving a minor technical challenge, discovering a useful open-source tool, building a custom utility, or gathering insights during engineering research, each piece is recorded and connected over time.

In particular, exploring emerging workflows—such as software development with AI agents—and documenting trial-and-error processes is just as valuable as the finished product.

Thus, each post on DrLeeWorks is both an independent article and **part of a growing, evolving archive of accumulated experience**.
